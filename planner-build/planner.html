Getting Started
How to Use This Plannerstart here
This planner is fully editable in Canva and print-ready in A4. Duplicate any page as many times as you need, type directly into every field, and make it your own. Here's how to get the most from it.
01
Open in Canva or Print
Use the free Canva link to edit on any device, or print the pages at home or at a print shop on A4 paper.
02
Duplicate Pages You Reuse
Trackers and planners are meant to repeat — right-click any page in Canva and duplicate it for each month, client or product.
03
Make It Yours
Swap the colours, fonts and photo frames for your own brand. Everything on every page is editable.
A Gentle Reminder
Progress over perfection. Fill in one page today — your future business will thank you.
